10 — Conversion Formulas
EV ↔ Lux
EV = log₂(Lux / 2.5)
Lux = 2.5 × 2^EV
Assumes reflected-light metering at ISO 100. Direct-light metering uses different constant.
Lumen ↔ Candela
lm = cd × Ω (steradians)
Point: lm = cd × 4π ≈ cd × 12.57
Spot: lm = cd × 2π(1 − cos θ)
θ = half-angle of spot cone
Foot-candle ↔ Lux
lx = fc × 10.764
fc = lx ÷ 10.764
100 fc ≈ 1,076 lx. US film/TV uses fc; metric DPs use lux.
Inverse Square Law
E₂ = E₁ × (d₁ / d₂)²

Double distance → ¼ lux
Triple distance → 1/9 lux
Applies in real, Octane, RS (Quadratic decay), and UE5. Disable in UE5 only if stylized look needed.
UE5 EV100
EV100 = log₂(N² / t × 100/S)
EV100 = log₂(Lux / 1.2)

Noon sun 100,000 lx → EV100 ≈ 16.4
Use Manual Exposure in Post Process for physical accuracy.
Octane / RS Power → Lux
No direct formula — relative.
Anchor camera EV to real-world
lux reference, scale lights to match.
Renderer "watts" are scene-relative. RS Physical Lights in lm/cd are closer to physical but still require camera calibration.
