UE5 Plugins
UE5 Plugin · Sky & Weather
Ultra Dynamic Sky
The fastest way to a fully realized sky system in UE5. Drop a single actor into your scene and you have atmospheric sky, volumetric clouds, time of day, weather states, lightning, and Lumen integration — all without wiring a single node. The range of looks you can achieve just by dialing weather and time of day is remarkable. The VRAM cost is real, but for cinematic work the creative speed is worth it.
Unreal Engine 5 Sky Atmosphere Volumetric Clouds Weather Lightning Lumen

Modular Asset Kits
3D Asset Kit · Multi-Genre
KitBash3D
High-quality modular 3D kits spanning sci-fi, fantasy, ancient, brutalist, and everything in between. The kits are designed to be mixed and layered — a single pack gives you enough pieces to build an entire environment. UE5-ready with Nanite support, and the "Secrets of the Luminara" kit in particular has been a go-to for temple and ancient structure work.
Modular Sci-Fi Fantasy Architecture Nanite UE5

Handcrafted 3D asset packs built for concept artists and environment designers. The "Big, Medium, Small" naming philosophy reflects their approach: every pack covers hero pieces, supporting elements, and fine detail props for a complete scene.

Scan Libraries
Photogrammetry · Scan Library
Scans Factory
Photorealistic scanned assets — surfaces, rocks, ground cover, organic debris, and architectural detail. The kind of material that grounds a CG environment in physical reality. Nanite-ready geometry means you drop them in at full resolution without LOD headaches. For cinematic work, scanned assets in the foreground are the difference between a render that reads as real and one that reads as CG.

Marketplaces
3D Marketplace · Industry Standard
TurboSquid
The largest 3D model marketplace in the world — and one I know well having worked there as a UI/UX Designer across PixelSquid, Kraken, StemCell, and brand initiatives. TurboSquid's catalogue spans every category imaginable, from photorealistic vehicles and characters to architectural assets and VFX elements. The StemCell certification program ensures assets meet a consistent quality and compatibility standard for production pipelines. For one-off assets that fall outside of kit-based workflows, it's the first stop.
